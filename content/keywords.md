# Keywords

The search terms transformpipe can honestly answer, grouped by what the person is trying to do rather than by
word shape — the grouping is what decides which article gets written and what it has to contain.

No volume or difficulty numbers are recorded here on purpose. They cannot be measured from this
repository, and a made-up number is worse than none: it survives longer than the guess it came from.
Run the list through whichever keyword tool you trust, then annotate it here.

Each cluster names the article that targets it. `content/blog/` holds them; a term that no article
targets is a gap, and that is the point of writing the two down side by side.

## What is not written yet

Four clusters above are marked as gaps: email and print, static site generators, where a document is
stored, and styling the output. They were scoped and then cut to keep the first twenty articles to
one idea each. They are the next four to write, in that order — the first two are the largest, and
the second two convert best, because someone asking where their file goes is deciding whether to
use the thing.

## How to use this list

One cluster, one article. An article that tries to cover two clusters ranks for neither and reads
like a brochure; an article per keyword produces twenty pages saying the same thing in different
words, which is worse.

Names in the first cluster are the hardest to rank for and the least likely to convert on their own —
someone searching "markdown to html" wants a box to drop a file into, not an essay. That page exists
already: it is the app. The articles are for the questions the app cannot answer in a dropzone.
